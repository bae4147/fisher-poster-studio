Fisher Poster Studio

Turn one of your papers into an interactive poster. Visitors click a panel to zoom in and hear you explain it, in an AI version of your own voice, and can ask an AI version of you questions about the paper.

Early version. Once a poster is made it cannot be edited yet. To change it, build it again: every build gets its own new link, and earlier posters stay up.

1Your paper

Upload the PDF of the published paper. AI reads it, including tables and figures, and drafts the poster panels and your narration scripts.

2About you

Used once to draw a pixel-art character of you for the poster and podcast, then discarded. Skip it and a default character is used. A clear, front-facing head-and-shoulders photo works best.

3About you and your motivation (optional)

The poster opens with an About the author card, then a Motivation panel. Your paper says little about you, so a few sentences here make the card much better; if you leave it empty, the card shows only what the paper states (your name and affiliation). If you write something, the panel and your narration are built from your words (shortened if needed).

4Your voice

Add audio or video of you speaking: a lecture recording, a talk, or a voice memo. Use parts where only you are talking (no students, music or applause). Aim for 1 to 3 minutes in total; 30 seconds is the minimum and 5 minutes the maximum. Long files are fine: trim each one to the part you want, and the studio compresses it before sending.

Edit right on the poster:

  • Change text: click it and type.
  • Change an icon: click it and pick another.
  • Delete an item: hover over it and click the red ×.
  • Add an item: click a blue + Add button (findings, numbers, steps, tips, tags, model boxes).
  • Show a figure from your paper: use the Add a figure tab.
The narration and podcast don't update by themselves. After you change content, check the Scripts tab so your AI voice says the same thing as the poster.
